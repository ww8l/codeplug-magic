# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. This is a hobby project about programming radios; nothing here is
worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about a byte offset, and someone is simply
  wrong rather than acting in bad faith.
- Be patient with people who are new to the hobby, to radios, or to software. "Read the
  manual" is not an answer.
- Say plainly when something is unverified. Claiming hardware behaviour you have not
  observed wastes other people's radio time and can damage their equipment — in this
  project that matters more than tone.
- Respect other people's data. Do not post someone's call sign, licence record, location or
  codeplug without their consent, and do not ask a contributor to attach a dump from a
  programmed radio to a public thread.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, or publishing
others' private information. Call signs are a small world; behave accordingly.

## Reporting

Raise a concern privately with the maintainer (**@ww8l** on GitHub) rather than in a public
thread. Reports will be handled discreetly.

The maintainer may edit, delete or lock comments, and block accounts, at their discretion.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages —
and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
